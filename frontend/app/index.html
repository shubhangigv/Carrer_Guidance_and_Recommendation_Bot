Final-year project

Find the career that fits you, in every field

Rate your interests, skills and subjects. Pathfinder compares you with every career in O*NET, explains each match from your own answers, and shows what to learn next.

How it works

  1. Create an account

    Your answers and results are saved so you can come back to them.

  2. Answer 22 short questions

    6 interests, 8 skills and 8 subjects. “No experience” and “Can’t assess” are fine answers.

  3. See your matches

    Careers from different fields, the reasons for each, and the skills to build.

Your data

Your password is stored only as a bcrypt hash. Your answers are used only to compute your matches for this final-year project.
