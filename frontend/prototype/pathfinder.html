Final-year project prototype

Find the career that fits you, in every field

Rate your subjects, interests and skills. Pathfinder compares you with every career in its database, explains each match from your own answers, and shows what to learn next.

How it works

  1. Create your profile

    Education, degree and year.

  2. Rate yourself

    Subjects, 18 interest statements, 16 skills and what matters to you in a job.

  3. See your matches

    Each career gets a Profile Match Score out of 100 and the reasons behind it.

  4. Close the gaps

    Pick a goal and follow a roadmap built from your skill gaps.

    How the score works
    
          
    For each career, three parts are compared with your answers using profile correlation (do your strongest areas match what the career rates most important?).
    
          
    Skills 30 · Interests 25 · Subjects 15, rescaled to 100. Work style, preferences and job-market data join the score in the next phase.
    
          
    Skill gaps compare O*NET's required level (0–7) with your self-rating mapped onto the same scale.
